FAQ

Questions, Answered.

Are the boots made from genuine leather?
Yes — every pair is crafted from 100% premium genuine leather sourced from India's finest tanneries and finished by our master artisans.
Do you ship internationally?
Absolutely. We deliver to 42+ countries via tracked express couriers. Duties are typically prepaid so there are no surprises.
How do I place an order?
Tap any "Order on WhatsApp" button and our team will personally assist with size, customisation and payment options.
What is the return policy?
Unused pairs may be returned within 14 days of delivery for exchange or refund. Custom sizes are final sale.
Can I customise size or colour?
Yes — bespoke commissions are our specialty. Message us on WhatsApp with your requirements.
How long does a pair last?
Cared for correctly, a WalkinChelsea pair lasts 5–10 years. We also offer resoling services.
